Numbers in a line, like a number line, help children see that each one is one more (or ten more) than the last. The voice counts along the carriages and stops at the gap. Counting in 10s usually comes after counting to 20 in 1s; 5s and 2s are usually a little later still. Every 5 trains earns a star, and each star moves up a level.
