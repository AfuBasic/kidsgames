Five Little Ducks

Ducks swim away over the hill. How many are left?

Settings for grown-ups
Ducks

Based on the counting song. Ducks swim away over the hill (taking away); in "Away and back", Mother Duck quacks and some come back (adding). The ducks stay in rows of five, so amounts are easy to see, and children can count what's left. After each answer the voice counts the ducks on the pond. There are always three numbers to choose from. Every 5 right answers earns a star, and each star moves up a level.
