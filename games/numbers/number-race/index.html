Number Race

Spin, then hop along the numbers. First to the end wins!

Based on studies where preschoolers who played a simple number board game in a straight line (1 to 10, saying each number as they moved) got better at numbers; a round board didn't help the same way. Your child taps to hop one square at a time and the voice says each number, so they count on along the line. Bigger boards use rows of ten, like a hundred square. Play together when you can: the studies had an adult alongside. Turns are always equal: if the first player reaches the end, the other gets one last turn, and if they get there too it's a draw. The score is kept on this device for each pair of names; tap End on the scoreboard to see who won the game and start a new one. A win or a draw earns a star.
