Number bonds: the pairs that make 5 and 10 (7 and 3 make 10). The egg box is a five- or ten-frame, so children can see the empty spaces. After each answer the missing eggs drop in one by one and the voice says the bond. Knowing bonds to 5, and some to 10, is a typical goal by age 5. Three numbers to choose from, never next to each other. Every 5 boxes earns a star, and each star moves up a level.
